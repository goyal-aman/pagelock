# PageLock Loom Demo Script

**Duration: 60-90 seconds**

---

## Opening (5 seconds)
"Hey! I'm Aman, and this is PageLock - a tool to password-protect any link with zero-knowledge encryption."

---

## The Problem (10 seconds)
"Ever needed to share a private Google Drive link or confidential document, but didn't want to trust a third-party service with your data? That's exactly why I built this."

---

## Demo Part 1: Creating a Protected Link (20 seconds)

**[Screen: Show PageLock homepage]**

"Here's how it works. Let's say I want to share this private document."

**[Action: Paste a URL - use a real Google Drive or Dropbox link]**

"I paste the URL here..."

**[Action: Enter password - type something like "demo123"]**

"...set a password..."

**[Action: Click 'Lock URL']**

"...and click Lock URL."

**[Action: Show the generated encrypted link]**

"PageLock gives me this encrypted link. Notice how it looks completely different from the original - that's AES-256 encryption happening right in my browser."

---

## Demo Part 2: Unlocking (15 seconds)

**[Action: Copy the link and open in new tab/incognito]**

"Now when someone receives this link..."

**[Screen: Show unlock page]**

"...they see this unlock page."

**[Action: Enter the password]**

"They enter the password I shared with them..."

**[Action: Click unlock and show the revealed URL or redirect]**

"...and boom! They get access to the original link."

---

## The Key Differentiator (15 seconds)

**[Optional: Show browser DevTools Network tab or just speak over the UI]**

"Here's the cool part: everything happens in your browser. The password and original URL are encrypted client-side before anything is stored. 

This means I literally cannot decrypt your links without the password. Zero-knowledge encryption."

---

"Perfect for:
- Sharing private documents with clients
- Distributing beta access links
- Sending confidential resources to your team
- Anything that needs password protection"

---

## Closing (10 seconds)

"It's completely free, no signup required, and your data stays private.

Try it at pagelock.top - I'd love to hear your feedback!"

**[Screen: Show pagelock.top URL clearly]**

---

## 🎬 Recording Tips

1. **Keep it snappy** - Aim for 60-75 seconds max
2. **Use a real example** - Actual Google Drive link makes it relatable
3. **Show, don't tell** - Let the product speak for itself
4. **Smile in your voice** - Sound enthusiastic but natural
5. **Clear audio** - Use a good mic, quiet room
6. **Clean browser** - Close unnecessary tabs, use incognito for demo
7. **Smooth cursor movements** - Don't rush, but don't linger

## 📋 Pre-Recording Checklist

- [ ] Test the full flow beforehand
- [ ] Have your demo URL ready to paste
- [ ] Clear browser cache/use incognito
- [ ] Close unnecessary tabs
- [ ] Check audio levels
- [ ] Prepare a short password (easy to type on camera)
- [ ] Have pagelock.top open and ready

## 🎯 Key Message

**"Zero-knowledge encryption means your secrets stay yours"** - Make sure this lands!
